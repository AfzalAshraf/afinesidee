Install on TV = works without this computer.

"Install on TV" registers a launcher tile directly on your VIDAA TV over MQTT/TLS. Once installed, the tile stays on your TV and opens the app even when Sidee is switched off. The hosted /dashboard page is an alternative single tile that needs Sidee running.

🕹️
"I can open an app but can't click inside it"

A TV remote is a D-pad, not a mouse. Normal desktop sites such as youtube.com open fine but you cannot click around inside them — that is the site, not Sidee, and not an ad blocker. Tiles marked 🖱 needs pointer need a TV-friendly URL (set it in Edit, e.g. youtube.com/tv), your TV browser's pointer/mouse mode, or the native app. Tiles marked 🕹 D-pad OK (Nuvio, Stremio, the Sidee dashboard) work with arrows + Enter out of the box.

Privacy & security

All TV communication stays on your local network over MQTT/TLS port 36669. Pairing always requires the PIN shown on the TV. Dashboard access is protected by the private key in its URL. There is no telemetry, account or cloud service. Custom URLs are opened by the TV — only add addresses you trust.
